Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Resolved IT service?

We provide IT support and security systems across the Southern Highlands of NSW, including Bowral, Mittagong, Moss Vale, Bundanoon, Robertson and Goulburn.

What IT services do you offer?

Everything from ad-hoc technical support and managed IT services to servers and networking, backups and disaster recovery, virus and malware removal, cyber security, hardware procurement, cloud solutions and security alarm systems.

Do you offer ongoing managed IT support or just one-off fixes?

Both. We handle one-off fixes for businesses that just need a problem solved, and offer proactive managed plans for businesses that want issues caught and monitored before they cause downtime.

How quickly can you respond to an IT issue?

Most remote issues are picked up the same day, and we can be onsite quickly across the Southern Highlands when hands-on support is needed. Managed clients get proactive monitoring so many problems are caught before they're noticed.

Do you provide security systems and alarm installation?

Yes. We hold a Master Security License and design, integrate and install alarm systems, CCTV and access control for businesses across the region.

How much does IT support cost?

It depends on your setup and whether you want ad-hoc or managed support. Get in touch for a free, no-obligation chat and we'll talk through what fits your business and budget.

Can you help set up a new computer or server?

Yes — we source and supply computers, laptops, servers and networking equipment at competitive prices, and handle the full setup and installation.

Do you help with backups and disaster recovery?

Yes. Most businesses we meet don't have a reliable, tested backup plan. We set up backups that are regularly restore-verified so you know they'll actually work if you need them.

Can you support us remotely, or do you need to be onsite?

Most day-to-day support can be handled remotely or over the phone, and we come onsite for anything that needs hands-on attention — hardware faults, installations, or more complex network work.
